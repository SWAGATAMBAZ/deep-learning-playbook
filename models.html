Universal tuning order (works for almost every model)

✅ When a card says "tune", tune in this order
  1. Data and labels: fix leakage, label noise, and the validation split (time-based or group-based).
  2. Learning rate (log-scale search) and schedule (warm-up + cosine).
  3. Capacity: model size, depth, width, input resolution or sequence length.
  4. Regularisation: weight decay, dropout/drop-path, augmentation, early stopping.
  5. Loss matched to the business cost (weighted, focal, quantile, ranking).
  6. Inference: threshold, calibration, TTA, ensembling, then distillation for speed.
