Talking about your DL project (the question you'll definitely get)

✅ A structure that works
  1. Business problem and metric: "Card fraud was costing ₹X/month; the goal was recall at 0.5% false-decline rate."
  2. Baseline: "LightGBM on 120 velocity features gave PR-AUC 0.41."
  3. Why deep learning: "Fraud showed up in the sequence of transactions, which aggregates were losing, so I added a GRU over the last 50 transactions."
  4. Key decisions and trade-offs: loss (focal), validation (out-of-time), latency (distilled, ONNX, 8 ms p95), explainability (SHAP on the hybrid).
  5. Result in business terms: "+6 pts recall at the same FPR ≈ ₹Y/month saved; A/B tested for 4 weeks."
  6. What you'd do next / what went wrong: shows maturity.
