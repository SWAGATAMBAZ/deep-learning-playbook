Industry playbook

Interviewers at a bank don't want "I'd use a transformer". They want the problem, the data, the model and why, the metric the business cares about, and the trap. Here's that for each major industry. Pick your target industry's tab and learn it cold.

The universal answer template

✅ Use this structure for any "how would you build X at our company" question
  1. Business goal and constraint: what decision does the model drive, and what does a wrong prediction cost (false positive vs false negative)? Latency? Regulation?
  2. Data: what's available at prediction time, how labels are created, delay and bias in labels.
  3. Baseline: rules or a GBDT. Then why DL (sequences, text, images, graphs, scale).
  4. Model and training: architecture, loss, imbalance handling, validation split.
  5. Metrics: offline metric tied to the business metric, then an online A/B test with guardrails.
  6. Deploy and monitor: latency, drift, retraining, explainability, fairness.
