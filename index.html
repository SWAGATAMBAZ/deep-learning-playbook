The 8-week roadmap

New: two extra computer-vision lessons and a 62-model encyclopedia with parameters and tuning advice for every model.

About 1–1.5 hours a day. Each step is ticked automatically when you mark its page as done. The order matters: everything after week 2 reuses the vocabulary from weeks 1–2.

WEEK 1

Neural-net foundations

Neuron → layers → activations → loss → gradient descent → backprop. Write a 2-layer net in PyTorch and know what every line does.

WEEK 2

Training & improving models

Optimizers, learning rate, init, BatchNorm, dropout, early stopping, class imbalance, and a debugging checklist. This is the most-asked area.

WEEK 3

Deep learning on tabular data

Why GBDTs usually still win, entity embeddings, Wide&Deep, TabNet, FT-Transformer. This is the bread and butter of banks and fintech.

WEEK 3

CNNs & vision

Convolution, pooling, ResNet skip connections, transfer learning. Used for KYC documents, product images and defect detection.

WEEK 3–4

CV II: Detection, segmentation & more

IoU, NMS, mAP; YOLO vs Faster R-CNN vs DETR; U-Net, Mask R-CNN, SAM 3; OCR, tracking, face match; DINOv3 and CLIP.

WEEK 4

CV III: Vision performance playbook

Diagnose by symptom, data levers, augmentation (live), the modern fine-tuning recipe, hyperparameter table, backbone choice, TTA, quantisation.

WEEK 4

Sequences I: RNN

Sequence task shapes, the recurrence, BPTT, vanishing/exploding gradients, PyTorch shapes, padding and packing, variants.

WEEK 4

Sequences II: LSTM

Cell state and three gates with a live cell simulator, why gradients survive, parameters, variants, tuning, common bugs, fraud and forecasting designs.

WEEK 4

Sequences III: GRU

Update and reset gates with a live simulator, LSTM vs GRU comparison, GRU4Rec, GRU-D, how to choose.

WEEK 4–5

Attention & Transformers

Q/K/V, why we divide by √d, multi-head attention, positional encoding, BERT vs GPT. Expect at least one question on this in every loop.

WEEK 5

Embeddings & NLP

Word2Vec → contextual embeddings, tokenization, fine-tuning BERT for complaints, intent and NER.

WEEK 6

Recommender systems

Matrix factorisation, two-tower retrieval, ranking models, sequential recommenders, NDCG. Central to e-commerce interviews.

WEEK 6

Time-series forecasting

LSTM, TCN, DeepAR, TFT, N-BEATS, PatchTST, foundation models, and walk-forward validation (no leakage).

WEEK 7

Anomaly detection & GNNs

Autoencoders for fraud, graph neural nets for fraud rings and AML.

WEEK 7

Generative AI & LLMs

VAE, GAN and diffusion in one page each; then LLMs, RAG, LoRA and evaluation. Now asked in most DS loops.

WEEK 8

Industry playbook + model chooser

Fintech, banking, e-commerce, insurance, healthcare and more: the problem, the model, the metric and the trap for each.

REFERENCE

Model encyclopedia

62 models across every family: intuition, key parameters with starting values, how to improve, when to use or avoid, and the interview point.

WEEK 8

Production & explainability

Serving, latency, drift monitoring, SHAP, fairness for lending. This is what separates a DS II answer from a DS I answer.

🎯 What DS II interviews test

DS II loops rarely ask you to derive things from scratch. They test judgement: Why this model over XGBoost? How did you handle imbalance? What would you check if validation loss went up? How would you serve it under 50 ms? How do you explain it to a regulator? Every page ends with those trade-offs, so learn those, not the proofs.
